How the monitor works

Wolt promotions are not all equally useful. The monitor turns raw promotion data into a cleaner comparison layer.

01

Collect

We read public Wolt venue and promotion data for monitored cities and keep a compact snapshot.

02

Normalize

Percentage discounts, basket discounts, multibuy offers and delivery promos are classified into comparable fields.

03

Rank

A value score favors broad, meaningful discounts and demotes weaker or highly conditional offers.

FAQ

About the data

Is this an official Wolt website?

No. Wolt Discount Monitor is an independent, open-source project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Wolt.

How fresh are the deals?

Monitored city snapshots use a one-hour cache. The exact snapshot time is shown above. If a city is not actively monitored, its data can be older or unavailable.

What does “best value” mean?

It is an estimate used for sorting, not a guarantee of savings. The score considers discount size, minimum-spend conditions, offer scope and promotion type.

Why can a deal differ from what I see in Wolt?

Promotions can change, expire, depend on account eligibility or differ by location. Always confirm the final price and conditions in Wolt before ordering.
